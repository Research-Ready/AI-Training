Practical AI Literacy for Teams

This repository contains training materials for a practical AI literacy workshop. The goal is to help teams understand AI without hype, recognize risks, identify useful use cases, and create a first responsible-use agreement.

The first product version is a 4-hour workshop for mixed teams, researchers, educators, SMEs, and knowledge workers.

Participants leave with:

a shared understanding of AI basics
a map of useful, risky, and unsuitable AI use cases
a responsible AI checklist
a first team AI working agreement
one concrete next step

Key product risks
Risk 1: Too much content

The existing material is broad. The first product must not try to cover everything.

Mitigation:

Focus only on practical AI literacy, risks, use cases, basic prompting, and team agreement.

Risk 2: Too generic

AI training is a crowded market.

Mitigation:

Use the positioning:

Practical AI Literacy for Teams

Make the output clear:

use-case map, responsible-use checklist, team agreement.

Risk 3: Too technical

Participants may not be technical.

Mitigation:

Avoid model architecture, coding, local AI setup, and complex tool comparisons.

Risk 4: Too much support expectation

Clients may expect technical help after the session.

Mitigation:

State clearly what is included and what is not included.

Implementation is a separate product.

Risk 5: No clear output

Awareness alone is weak.

Mitigation:

Every session must end with a concrete artifact.

Risk 6: Hybrid delivery fails

Hybrid sessions often exclude remote participants.

Mitigation:

Only offer hybrid with good audio, shared digital board, and a co-facilitator.

Reasons:

harder to explain
